Free Brat Generator for custom text images

A Brat Generator turns your words into a simple, bold image inspired by the deliberately imperfect visual language associated with the brat era. Instead of recreating a design manually in a graphics editor, you can type a phrase, choose a background, tune the blur, and export an image in the dimensions you need. The live canvas shows the same composition used for the download, so you can judge line breaks, contrast, and spacing before saving anything.

This tool is useful when you want a quick social post, playlist cover, party invitation, reaction image, announcement, or personal graphic. The familiar green-and-black combination is available immediately, but it is not the only option. White, black, pink, transparent, and fully custom colors help the same compact text treatment fit a wider range of ideas. The result is an independent fan-style design rather than official album artwork, and the generator does not include logos or copyrighted lyrics.

Brat colors, font style, and blur

The default green preset uses #8ACE00, paired with dark text. That hex value is a practical digital approximation used by many fan-made graphics; screens and color profiles can still make it appear slightly different from device to device. For a reversed design, choose black with white text. Pink adds a brighter pop direction, while a white background is useful when the image needs to sit comfortably inside a light page or document. Custom color controls let you go beyond those presets without changing the rest of the composition.

The lettering uses a condensed sans-serif treatment selected for browser reliability and adjusted to create a narrow, direct appearance. A small amount of blur softens the edges, while the pixelated option makes them rougher. These controls are stylistic approximations, not a claim that the page distributes an exact official font. Start with the default blur, then reduce it for crisp small graphics or raise it gently when a larger export feels too polished. Readability should remain the priority, especially for long phrases.

Export sizes, formats, and transparent images

Choose a square canvas for feed posts and cover art, a 9:16 canvas for stories and vertical screens, or 16:9 for video thumbnails and wide banners. Square output is adjustable from 512 to 2048 pixels. Vertical and widescreen modes use common 1080 × 1920 and 1920 × 1080 dimensions, which reduces the need for resizing after download. Every platform may crop previews differently, so keep important words away from the outer edge and check the result in its actual destination.

PNG is the best general-purpose choice for sharp text and is required when you want a fully transparent background. WebP can preserve transparency at a smaller file size in modern workflows. JPEG is broadly compatible and useful for finished solid-color images, but it cannot store transparency. If a transparent preset is active, choose PNG or WebP instead. Copy image creates a PNG in browsers that expose secure clipboard image access; when that feature is unavailable, the regular download button is the reliable fallback.

Writing text that works in the layout

Short lowercase phrases tend to produce the clearest result because the words can occupy more of the canvas. Longer text is supported through wrapping and automatic fitting, and intentional line breaks can help control the rhythm. If a sentence becomes too small, shorten it, switch to a taller aspect ratio, or divide the thought across lines. The right and justified alignment options can create a different energy, but centered text remains the most predictable choice across square, vertical, and wide images.

You may enter your own lyric-like phrase, caption, or title, but the generator does not supply copyrighted lyrics. Only publish text you created, text in the public domain, or material you have permission to use. The same principle applies to commercial work: exporting an image does not transfer rights to someone else’s words, name, music, branding, or likeness. Treat the tool as a layout utility, then review the final image for the context where it will appear.

Private, local image generation

The preview and export are rendered with browser canvas technology on your device. Your typed content does not need to be sent to an image-generation service, and no signup is required. This makes experimentation fast and avoids storing a project on our servers. Basic site analytics may record that a tool action occurred, but the text you type and the pixels in your canvas are masked from session analytics and are not included in tool event labels.

Because rendering happens locally, results can vary slightly with browser font support and image clipboard permissions. Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari can download generated images, while image copying may be more limited on mobile or inside in-app browsers. If a copied image does not appear, download the PNG and share the saved file instead. Keep a copy of any important export because the page does not maintain a cloud project history.

Brat Generator FAQ

Can I make a brat image with a transparent background?

Yes. Choose the Transparent preset and export PNG or WebP to preserve transparency. JPEG does not support transparent pixels.

What is the brat green color?

The green preset in this independent fan-style tool uses #8ACE00. You can also open the custom background color control and choose a different shade.

What font does the Brat Generator use?

The tool uses a legally available condensed sans-serif treatment with adjustable blur to create a brat-inspired look. It does not claim to provide an exact official album font.

Can I create black, white, or pink brat text images?

Yes. Use the black, white, and pink presets, or combine any custom background and text colors.

Can I use long text or lyrics?

The generator wraps and scales longer text, but short phrases are usually easier to read. Only enter lyrics or other material that you wrote or have permission to use.

Does the Brat Generator work on mobile?

Yes. The editor is designed for current mobile and desktop browsers. Available copy-image features can vary by browser, but image download remains the dependable option.

Is my text or image uploaded?

No. Text rendering and image creation happen locally in your browser. The tool does not require an account and does not upload your canvas to create a download.

Is this an official Brat or Charli XCX tool?

No. This is an independent fan-style generator and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Charli XCX, her team, or her record labels.
